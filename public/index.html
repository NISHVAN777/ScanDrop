Everything you need to share files safely

Built for quick handovers between phones, laptops and friends, without giving up your privacy.

End-to-end encryption

Files are locked in your browser before they leave. The key lives only in the QR code, so not even our server can open them.

Direct device to device

Send straight to a nearby phone over Wi-Fi with no upload and no size limit. Great for big videos.

Links that delete themselves

Choose 10 minutes to 7 days, or a one-time link. When time is up, the file is gone for good.

PIN protection

Add a 4 to 8 digit PIN for extra safety. Five wrong tries locks the file for 10 minutes.

Never start over

Big uploads go in small pieces. If the Wi-Fi drops or the tab closes, pick the file again and it continues.

Share the code anywhere

Show the QR on screen, or send it as an image on WhatsApp, Telegram, email or SMS.

Your files stay yours

ScanDrop is designed so that we can't look inside what you share, even if we wanted to.

  • Encrypted on your deviceEvery file gets its own random AES-256 key before it uploads.
  • The key never reaches usIt travels only inside the QR link, in a part browsers never send to servers.
  • Even the name is hiddenThe server only sees a scrambled file called encrypted.bin.
  • Gone when you say soExpired and one-time files are deleted automatically.

Questions people ask

Do I need to install an app or make an account?

No. ScanDrop runs in the browser. The person receiving just points their phone camera at the QR code.

How big can my file be?

Up to 2 GB with Upload & share. With Send directly there is no limit, because the file goes straight from your device to theirs.

Can ScanDrop see my files?

Not when end-to-end encryption is on (it is by default). The file is encrypted on your device and the key is only inside the QR link, which never reaches the server.

How long do files stay online?

You choose: 10 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days, and you can limit the number of downloads. You can also delete a file early.

What if the upload stops halfway?

Nothing is lost. Press Resume, or pick the same file again later, and ScanDrop continues from where it stopped.

Why does Send directly need the page open?

Because the file travels live from your device to theirs. If you close the page, the transfer stops. Use Upload & share if they will download it later.
